The narrator, Lloyd, is carried and tended somewhere unnamed in the gap between the burning Ascension House and the let room ch. 17 opens in, his delirium sliding without warning between the salt-crusted Stillwell stair, the Vantry sub-basement stair, and the broken-roofed stair at his mother's house in Callow Ford, the same rail running under his hand through all three. Ivet surfaces in one of Ascension House's brine tanks and asks, mildly, why he never came home, while fragments of the memory he surrendered at the chalice — tallow and cold stone, a texture that isn't quite rope, his own name repeated into noise — refuse to assemble into anything he can keep. An unplaced voice says Crane's name once, conversationally, and a separate courteous man asks after someone else's safety, both passing him unremarked among the night's other wrong noises, while the knot-cord in his hands counts out to ninety-one knots instead of the nineteen he remembers and agrees with itself twice over. Maria's voice reaches him from outside the slide at intervals, naming a cup, a window, a blanket, her own hand, and the chapter ends mid-slide, before he wakes, on the half-inch gap of daylight through his mother's north gable.
