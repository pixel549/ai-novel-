Lloyd, possessing a supernatural Insight, perceives anomalies and a hidden door that the narrator cannot see, but he cannot interact with them until Maria verbally confirms their reality. After Maria validates Lloyd’s perception, he physically pushes through the solid basalt wall, opening a passage that remains invisible and solid to the narrator. The narrator tests this dynamic by claiming a non-existent lever exists on the floor, but Maria denies its reality, causing Lloyd to ignore the narrator’s statement entirely. This interaction establishes the group’s operational hierarchy: Lloyd provides visions, Maria validates them as fact, and the narrator serves as the grounded baseline for physical safety and measurement.
