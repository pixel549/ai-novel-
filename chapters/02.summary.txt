Edie, Lloyd, and Maria descend into a subterranean salt mine where Edie suffers severe temporal distortion and hunger hallucinations due to the environment's warping effects. They encounter a wire-like entity in a granite chamber, which Lloyd attempts to strike while Maria uses Arcana to pin it against the wall. Edie’s “Still Mind” ability allows her to anticipate a structural shockwave that destroys the creature, leaving the team physically battered but intact. After confirming the preceding tunnel is stable, Maria secures a knot-cord to an iron drainpipe to mark their position and depth for future navigation.
