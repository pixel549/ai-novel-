The narrator, Lloyd, and the others take a night's freelance job at Hessel Bight, clearing a flooded cellar of eleven war-dead beneath a burnt manse in exchange for bread and a roof. While Lloyd works a stuck drain latch, his second, unseen arm draws a length of iron out of a solid wall that Edie's structural survey — and her Still Mind — never registers at all, leaving open whether it was ever really there for her to miss. Upstairs, a weary woman recites the going rates for odd jobs while Edie prices the work, and outside, Maria spends hours setting a boy's crushed hand and is called "Witch" rather than "Sister," a label she lets stand without correcting. The chapter closes with the three of them bedding down for the night above the cellar, the floodwater still lapping at its walls below.
