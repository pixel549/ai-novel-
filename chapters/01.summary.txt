Edie pays her entry at Stillwell Hydro with a ledger slip drawn against Senator Ferrick's household account, and the clerk stamps it and hands her a brass token marked "Run 1" without asking her name. In the waiting room she meets Lloyd, a heavy, broken-nosed labourer coiling rope, and Maria, a small burn-scarred woman laying out blankets, and the three are told the facility only runs patrons in groups of three. Maria explains the first-run procedure to Edie while Lloyd says little, and the three move into the intake chamber, where Lloyd opens the main valve, Maria lights the gas lamp, and Edie watches the pressure gauge climb as steam fills the lines. They are sealed into the steam room together as the chamber fills with vapor and the run begins.
