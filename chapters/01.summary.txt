Edie pays her entry at Stillwell Hydro with a ledger slip drawn against Senator Ferrick's household account, and the clerk stamps it and hands her a brass token marked "Run 1" without asking her name. In the waiting room she meets Lloyd, a heavy, broken-nosed labourer coiling rope, and Maria, a small burn-scarred woman laying out blankets, and the three are told the facility only runs patrons in groups of three. The three move into the intake chamber, where Lloyd opens the main valve, Maria lights the gas lamp, and Edie watches the pressure gauge climb before they are sealed into the steam room together for the ten-minute first charge. When the timer sounds they're let out into a cold corridor, towel off, and Edie pockets the token for next time, the chapter ending with her standing a moment in the hydro's doorway as the steam curls off her coat.
