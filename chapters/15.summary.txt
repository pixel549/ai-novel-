The narrator, Lloyd, Maria, and Edie execute Edie's plan to both destroy Ascension House and carry out the evidence — documents first, fire second, a route decided in advance — with Maria explaining that her fire is the wrong tool here, since any burst large enough to matter would kill the twelve people still trapped in the tanks, so lightning and sustained light become her real contribution instead. The retreat goes wrong when the bound thing from the sub-basement's last room gets loose and a shelf of homunculi proves viable after all, forcing Maria to use two precise, self-loathed lightning strikes while Edie takes a blow her foreknowledge couldn't help her dodge, and Lloyd holds the stair alone against three attackers he alone can perceive and is right about every time. Lloyd goes down with his skull, ribs, and right arm broken, and Maria chooses to carry him out through the burning building rather than heal him on the spot, a decision that will cost him. In the yard outside, Ondine Vail is waiting, and without raising her voice or looking at Lloyd once, she asks Maria, sincerely and without malice, whether she understands what she has just cost the world; Maria doesn't answer and carries Lloyd past her into the lane, and they get out with the papers but not intact.
