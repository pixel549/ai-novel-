The narrator, Maria, visits Ferrick's house to see Edie despite having already lost the argument about Crane, and finds Edie running his security with four unremarkable, drilled people and an iron cabinet — holding the real Vantry registers, untouched in two months — bolted into a former nursery. Ferrick himself is warm and immediately solicitous of her old burn scars, and proudly shows her the sub-panel's excellent, damning second report on the valley's water, which has produced four sub-panel sessions, no vote, and a hearing calendar ringed for next spring. Edie says nothing about any of it aloud, but Maria watches her tally the futility in silence, and her Still Mind flinches uselessly a half-second ahead of a dropped tray elsewhere in the house. Edie asks after Lloyd's arm, Maria gives her only "mending," and Edie — who has never once asked either of them for anything — doesn't press, though she plainly notices the gap in the answer.
