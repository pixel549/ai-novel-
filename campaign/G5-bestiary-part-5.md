# G5 — BESTIARY ADDITIONS, PART V

*Reader: DM. Builds on `R5a-old-coast-overview.md` and `R5b-old-coast-leads-
antagonist-endings.md` §5 (Encounters) — read both first; this file does not
restate the lead web, the set pieces, the endings, or Region V's geography,
it supplies the archetype write-ups §5 names and leaves undetailed. Cites
`R5a`, `R5b`, `C2-factions-clocks.md`, `G1-bestiary-part-1.md`, `G2-bestiary-
part-2.md`, `G3-bestiary-part-3.md`, `G4-bestiary-part-4.md`, `source-
bible.md`, `hooks.md`, and `novel/canon.md` §§2/4/5/7/8 throughout. No
`campaign/system/` ruleset exists yet (Q-1); every entry below is behaviour
and tells, never a stat block — numbers are generated live at the table,
archetype and tier only, marked `(SYS?)` per `A2`'s standing instruction.*

## How to use this file

Same job `G1`–`G4` did for Regions I–IV, now for the last region: the
unnamed face a lead-web route needs consistent behaviour for (**People**)
and the sea's own instance of the Attrition-scarred ground (**Pest-Class
Horrors**). Region V is the quietest Part in the campaign by design (`R5b`
§5: "a DM reaching for a roll here is reaching for the wrong tool"), and
this file is correspondingly the thinnest Bestiary entry yet — shorter even
than `G3`'s own deliberately thin one. Both of this Part's Church
operatives, Sister Edda Vance and Brother Elias Prynne, are already built
in full in `R5a` and `R5b` §6 respectively, with their own tells, voice, and
defeat/flight/capture states; nothing here duplicates either of them, and
this file explicitly does not add a third, generic Church-operative
archetype the way `G2`'s Watcher or `G4`'s Specialist Staff did, because
`R5b` §5 names no undetailed operative category left for it to cover. Read
an entry once, before the session it's needed in; neither of these is meant
to carry a scene the way Vance or Prynne do.

**Session default — D-39 (`DECISIONS.md`).** `R5b` §5 names two categories
without building them out — the unnamed village elder behind two of the
lead web's own routes, and a "Hollowmere fisherman's half-remembered ward
against drowning" — and `R5a` explicitly defers the Tide Flats' own
Pest Pressure Clock instance to this file ("`G5`'s job to build, not yet
written"). This session invents the behaviour and tells each needs, plus a
deliberate variety call on the Unregistered Arcana Practitioner's own
escalating-caution pattern (see below) — logged here rather than decided
silently, and overturnable the same way `D-18`, `D-33`, `D-35`, and `D-37`
were for Regions I–IV.

## People

### The Hollowmere Elder

Covers `R5b`'s Reveal A, Route 2 and Reveal B, Route 2 — explicitly the
same figure reused across both, per `R5b`'s own text ("the same old-timer
from Reveal A's Route 2, pressed a little further") — and, per `R5b`'s own
choice, "nobody this file names because the point is that it could be
almost anyone in a village this small." This file keeps that choice: the
archetype is deliberately unnamed, reusable as whichever specific net-
mender or retired boat-hand a table's own session produces.

**Behaviour.** Age is the qualification, not standing — this person
remembers further back than Catchpole's own account books go, which is the
whole reason Route 2 exists alongside Route 1's ledgers. They don't
understand what they remember any better than anyone else in Hollowmere
does; they've simply been alive long enough to have seen more of the
pattern than anyone younger has. They speak of "the House up at Fallow
Head" the way they'd speak of weather or the tide — a fact of life, not a
mystery they're sitting on — and they don't volunteer the fuller version
(Reveal B's "it didn't start with her and won't end with her either")
unless pressed a second time, specifically because it has genuinely never
occurred to them before that anyone would want to know.

**Tells.** Measures time by boats, seasons, and herring runs, not years or
dates — "that was the summer the herring didn't come," never a number.
Repeats a fragment almost word for word if asked about it twice, the way a
story gets worn smooth by decades of only ever being told to people who
already knew it. Shows no fear of the House and no real curiosity about
it either — sixty-plus years of Hollowmere's own incuriosity (`R5a`),
worn into one specific person rather than a village-wide habit.

**Where this matters.** This is the one NPC in this file that can carry a
whole short scene on its own, same caveat `G1`'s own introduction holds for
Corlett and Mirrin Tope rather than its other archetypes — but it is still
an archetype, not a named character `R5b` built, and a DM is free to put a
different face and voice on it for Reveal A and Reveal B if that reads
better at a given table than one recurring elder.

`(SYS?)` tier: non-combat; no stat block needed under any circumstance this
file anticipates.

### The Unregistered Arcana Practitioner, Hollowmere Instance (optional)

Covers `R5b` §5's optional Arcana encounter: "a Hollowmere fisherman's
half-remembered ward against drowning, worth a scene only if a PC's own
hook calls for it" — the fifth and last regional instance of the archetype
`G1`–`G4` each built their own version of for Regions I–IV.

**Behaviour.** Every earlier instance of this archetype got more guarded
than the one before it — `G1`'s valley practitioner minimised on instinct,
`G2`'s Lodging-Row Spark more so, `G3`'s Boarding-District Reader more
guarded again, and `G4`'s Fen instance "the fastest to the wariness of any
region's version yet." This file makes a deliberate variety call and
breaks that pattern rather than extending it a fifth time: the Hollowmere
fisherman plays the ward openly, in front of other villagers, without a
second thought — launching a boat with a murmured phrase half the village
has heard a hundred times and nobody has ever once reported anywhere,
because nobody out here has ever had occasion to. This isn't safety; it's
the same incuriosity that lets Catchpole's own sixty-year retainer go
unquestioned (`R5a`) extending, for the first time in this campaign, to
cover a minor native gift instead of a Church secret. The wariness `G1`–
`G4` all built in is still present underneath, just dormant rather than
habitual — the fisherman freezes immediately and completely if a stranger
with any Church bearing is nearby, the one tell this instance keeps from
every predecessor.

**Tells.** Speaks of the ward exactly the way Hollowmere speaks of the
retainer — a fact of life, not a secret — and will demonstrate it for a
party that asks kindly rather than presses. Can't explain where the gift
came from any more than Maria can explain her own (canon §2), the fifth
and last time this file's own family of archetypes cites that uncertainty
as its model.

**Where this matters, and its limits.** Skip entirely if no PC has the
hook, same restraint `G1`–`G4` all held. If used, a DM should not read this
instance's relative openness as evidence the Church's reach has shrunk out
here — `R5b` §6 proves otherwise: Prynne still arrives, just late and from
outside the region, once there's reason to. Hollowmere is undiscovered,
not undiscoverable, and this archetype's ease is a product of that, not a
contradiction of it.

`(SYS?)` tier: low, non-combat.

## Pest-Class Horrors (the Attrition-scarred ground)

Per `C2`, `G1`–`G4`: weather, not villains, with no wants in the faction
sense, worse wherever disturbed and otherwise dormant, never escalating
into a boss fight. Regions I–IV each gave this archetype a home address —
the mine's older workings, Vantry's foundations and culverts, Carrowgate's
kept ground, the Fen Line's trenches. Region V's is the sea itself: the
wreck of the troop transport *Aldermoor*, lost with most hands during the
Attrition and never salvaged, visible on the Tide Flats at the lowest tides
(`R5a`).

### The Tideline Lingerer

The same low-tier archetype `G1` built as the Collapsed-Tunnel Lingerer,
re-dressed a second time by `G2` as the Rail-Cut Lingerer, a third time by
`G3` as the Kept-Ground Lingerer, and a fourth time by `G4` as the diffuse
Fen-Line Lingerer — re-dressed a fifth and last time here for the
*Aldermoor*'s own wreck, per `G1`'s own instruction that a region doesn't
need two pest archetypes to make the same clock legible. Unlike `G4`'s
diffuse spread along the whole Fen Line, this instance is concentrated at
one site, same shape as `G1`–`G3`'s own versions — the *Aldermoor* is one
wreck, not a network of trenches, and the Tide Flats' own Secrets entry
(`R5a`) describes a single located pressure, not a scattered one.

**Behaviour.** Identical in kind to all four predecessors: doesn't hunt,
doesn't pursue past the wreck itself, investigates sluggishly rather than
attacking, and loses interest within minutes of a party moving off. No
plan, no grievance — a pest problem, not a haunting (canon §5), exactly as
`G1`–`G4` all held. **Bound by the tide, not by a corridor or a line, which
is this instance's one genuinely new wrinkle:** at low tide, when the hull
breaks the surface, it is reachable and active; as the tide comes back in
it withdraws below the waterline rather than following onto dry land — a
hard boundary none of `G1`–`G4`'s land-bound versions needed, and the
coast's own simplest safety valve for a party that doesn't want a fight.
Per `R5a`'s own Secrets entry: at low Pest Pressure, nothing is here to
find at all; at high pressure, this same archetype simply runs at a
heavier tier `(SYS?)` — there is no second, worse creature waiting
underneath this one, same single-archetype economy `G1` set for every
region since.

**Tells.** Ribs of the hull breaking the surface at the lowest tides
(`R5a`'s own tell, reused here as the archetype's own sign rather than
scenery); gulls that won't settle on that stretch of water even when the
fish are running, the clearest ambient warning on the whole coast; a creak
of corroded metal that doesn't match the pull of the current it's
supposedly moving with.

**Function in the Part.** Gives the sea the same "this ground has its own
reason to be left alone" texture the mine gave Region I, Vantry's
foundations gave Region II, the Senate's kept ground gave Region III, and
the Fen Line gave Region IV — and, per `R5a`'s own framing, proves that the
war's unhealed cost "reaches this coast too," the same point `C1`'s own
Scars-of-the-Attrition texture makes everywhere else. It touches nothing
at Fallow Head or Hollowmere and has no connection to the First House's own
record; it's weather, same as its four predecessors, and the tide itself
is `R5a` §4's own pacing lever for when a DM wants it to matter and when
not.

`(SYS?)` tier: low; a contained, tide-gated encounter, never a chase across
open water, per `C2`'s own instruction that this clock should never resolve
into a boss fight.

## Fourth Mercy Products — explicitly not built here

Unlike `G2`'s optional Tank-Four Remnant and `G4`'s confirmed Tanks/Early
Growth entries, this file builds no Fourth Mercy Products category, for the
same reason `G3` gave for Region III and not out of oversight: `R5b` §5
states plainly, "None. Per `R5a`'s own DM notes, Fallow Head runs no active
ascension work and never has within living memory; there is nothing here to
encounter, only a record of what used to be tried elsewhere." There is
accordingly nothing left for a bestiary entry to supply — the First House's
horror is entirely informational (`R5a` §5), and dressing it in a creature
or a tank-room prop would contradict the one restraint `R5a` and `R5b` both
hold hardest.

## DM Notes

**Vance and Prynne are not in this file, and shouldn't be run from it.**
Both are fully built, named, and voiced in `R5a` and `R5b` §6 with their own
tells and defeat/flight/capture states; treat them as that file's own
characters, not as this one's archetypes, and don't flatten either into a
generic "Church operative" for convenience.

**The Hollowmere Elder's openness and the Arcana instance's ease are both
about isolation, not safety.** Per `R5a`'s own premise card, the Church's
discretion out here is real and the money genuinely arrives — but Prynne's
own arrival in `R5b` §6 proves the reach still gets here eventually. Don't
let either archetype's relative unguardedness read as the Church being
weaker on this coast than anywhere else in the campaign; it's slower, not
weaker.

**Never let the Tideline Lingerer become a set piece.** `R5a` is explicit
that this region's only real danger is informational until `R5b` says
otherwise, and `R5b` never does — nothing here is meant to escalate into
the Part's climax. If a table wants a physical threat at the Old Coast,
reach for Prynne's own late, unhurried arrival (`R5b` §6) before reaching
for this archetype a second time in one session.

**None of this needs a fight to matter.** Per `A1` pillar 1 and the cost
test (canon §8), both archetypes above can resolve a scene without combat —
an elder who simply talks, a fisherman who demonstrates a ward and nothing
more, a Lingerer that withdraws with the tide. Reach for combat because the
table wants it that session, not because an entry exists and needs using.

**The novel's three stay offstage, same as `R5a`/`R5b`.** No change from
either file: Lloyd, Maria, and Edie do not appear in this Part, even
restrictively, and this file invents no reason to put them here.
