# J1 — RUMOUR AND ENCOUNTER TABLES, HANDOUTS

*Reader: DM (handouts below are player-facing once given out). Formally
depends on `C2-factions-clocks.md` and `R1b-larrow-valley-leads-
antagonist.md` per `PLAN.md`, but is written after the full Parts spine,
Bestiary, and Compendium (`R1a`–`R5b`, `G1`–`G5`, `H1`–`H5`), so it covers
all five regions rather than Region I alone — a session default, logged
below. Cites `source-bible.md`, `hooks.md`, `C2`, every `Ra`/`Rb` file's §5
(Encounters), and `novel/canon.md` §§3–4 throughout. No `campaign/system/`
ruleset exists yet (Q-1); nothing below needs one — these are selection
tables and handout text, never stat blocks or costs.*

## How to use this file

Two table types and a handful of handouts, the two things `PLAN.md`'s J
item promises and several files above have been pointing at without
building. **Rumour tables** give a DM eight things a party might overhear
in each region, truth-value marked for DM eyes only. **Encounter tables**
turn each `Ra`/`Rb` file's own Encounters list (§5) into something rollable,
keyed to `C2`'s Pest Pressure Clock (Clock 5) the way that clock's own entry
already asks this file to do. **Handouts** are the first verbatim,
player-facing text several earlier files named a prop for but left
unwritten — hand these over physically; don't summarise them.

Any die size works; tables below assume a d8 for rumours (eight entries
each) and a d8 for encounters, re-rolling where noted. Nothing here invents
a mechanic — `S1` owns mechanics, this file only owns selection.

## Rumour Tables

Roll or pick. **[True]** happened as stated; **[False]** didn't, and the
DM should know why it's going around; **[Partly true]** is worth letting
the party chase; **[Open]** is a hook the book itself never resolved — run
it either way, consistently, once decided.

### Region I — Larrow Valley

1. **[True]** "The Works pays two crowns for word of anyone asking after
   the old surveys." (see Handout 2 below)
2. **[False]** "Crane's own water poisoned him in the end, same as
   everyone else." He died in Carrowgate, by a hand, not a well
   (`source-bible.md`) — a comforting story the valley tells itself anyway.
3. **[Partly true]** "Two inspectors who pushed too hard got sent to the
   coast." One did; Garrick Fen is quietly near Stillwell instead
   (`R1b`, D-17).
4. **[True]** "There's a man wanted on every board from here to Harrow's
   Weir, and he's not who the notice says he is." (see Handout 3)
5. **[Open]** "Aldous up at Selwick never counts a day right." Whether
   he's wrong or the marker post is stays exactly as unresolved as
   `hooks.md` #10 and `T2` left it — a DM who's run `T2` already knows the
   consequence; one who hasn't can let this stand as a mundane oddity.
6. **[Partly true]** "Pellick won't see clean water before the frost."
   Likely true per `C2`'s Capping Clock; let the Capping-Camp Tester
   (`G1`) confirm or correct it if asked directly.
7. **[False]** "The old manse cellar at Hessel Bight was cleared properly;
   there's nothing left down there." `R1a` leaves this deliberately open —
   a DM running `G1`'s Collapsed-Tunnel Lingerer there should treat this
   rumour as wrong, quietly.
8. **[True]** "Mirrin Tope won't let the Mercy brothers near her grandson."
   She hasn't, and won't; it isn't about the brothers (`R1a`).

### Region II — Vantry

1. **[True]** "Ascension House still smells of smoke, a year on, if the
   wind's wrong." The ruin stands; nothing rebuilt it (`R2a`, D-21).
2. **[False]** "Everyone inside Ascension House died in the fire." Several
   didn't; the ward's own survivors are exactly what the standing archive
   (`hooks.md` #3) still holds unread records of.
3. **[True]** "A lodger went missing from the Shuttle and nobody's found
   her." Nell Quane; her fate stays a table's own call (`R2b`, D-21).
4. **[True]** "Brother Wick never turns away anyone who's actually hungry."
   His tell, exactly as built (`R2b`).
5. **[Partly true]** "The broadsheet story about Ascension House was
   bought off after eleven days." Nobody bought it off; the Press Cycle
   (`C2` Clock 3) simply ran its own course, same as it's built to.
6. **[Open]** "Something in the old lab's burned-out wing still moves."
   `G2`'s Tank-Four Remnant, if the DM has chosen to make the possibility
   real this session — optional and never confirmed outright (`hooks.md`
   #7).
7. **[True]** "The city assembly still has the old intake register, if you
   know to ask for it by date." `hooks.md` #3 — true, and almost never
   acted on.
8. **[False]** "Coal Drop Yards is cursed since the Attrition." Ordinary
   superstition; the Pest Pressure Clock's real address here is
   foundations and the rail cutting (`R2a`), not the yards themselves.

### Region III — Carrowgate

1. **[True]** "The Church paid for Crane's death without a fight." The
   Sanctum's restitution, fast and uncontested — not an admission of guilt
   (`R3b`, D-23).
2. **[False]** "The Church actually did it." The public story most of the
   capital now believes; canon already settles who killed Crane, and it
   wasn't the Church (`R3b`).
3. **[True]** "Nobody at the Indemnity Office sees the same carpet men
   twice." True, and exactly as unremarkable as it sounds, until it isn't
   (`R3b` — The Carpet Men).
4. **[Partly true]** "A man and a woman who sound like they're from the
   valley have been seen near the boarding district." Rumour and
   resemblance only, per `A1`'s strictest-region restraint — never a
   sighting, never a scene (`R3a`).
5. **[True]** "Everyone at the Waiting House is waiting on that office for
   something." Caddock Lowe's whole tenancy, in one line (`R3a`, D-22).
6. **[False]** "The Senate dome is hollow; you can hear the chamber from
   the street." Texture only, no play use.
7. **[True]** "Ask a Sanctum clerk about Crane's case and they'll tell you
   straight that it's closed." Not evasion — the institutional truth, as
   the Sanctum understands it (`R3b`).
8. **[False]** "There's a tunnel under the Sanctum that comes up in the
   Senate cellars." Nothing on the page supports this; a DM who wants
   Church reach to feel larger than it admits should use `hooks.md` #8
   (Vail's speed) instead, not this.

### Region IV — Lissom Fen

1. **[True]** "The House of Quiet Rest takes men nobody else will keep."
   Its genuine cover, exactly as built (`R4a`).
2. **[Partly true]** "Greymere sells more than a nerve cure, if you ask the
   right way." True of Josiah Rede specifically; he sells access, not
   answers (`R4b`, D-25).
3. **[True]** "A corporal went in for a rest cure and never came back out
   right." Corporal Emrys Dunmore; his fate stays open (`R4b`, D-25).
4. **[False]** "The Fen Line's hauntings started at the House." They
   didn't — Attrition-era ground, diffuse along the old trench line, with
   nothing to do with the House above it (`R4a`; `G4`'s Fen-Line
   Lingerer).
5. **[True]** "Rufus Pledge keeps a slate at the Night Siding, same as any
   lodging house." (`R4a`, D-24)
6. **[Partly true]** "Letters keep coming for a man at the House who isn't
   answering them." Teyla Dunmore's unanswered letters to her brother
   (`H4`).
7. **[True]** "Ask after anyone by name at the House and you'll get a kind
   answer that tells you nothing." Sister Anstey's genuine, unknowing
   charity (`R4b`).
8. **[False]** "Greymere's water is cursed, same as the valley's." No —
   Greymere is the supply link, not the disease; don't conflate the two
   even as a rumour (`R4a`, D-24).

### Region V — the Old Coast

1. **[True]** "Hollowmere keeps itself to itself, and likes it that way."
   Its quiet complicity, stated plainly (`R5b`).
2. **[Partly true]** "Sister Vance has kept the First House's books sixty
   years and never shown them to a living soul." True until this Part —
   she will, on her own terms (`R5a`/`R5b`, D-26/D-27).
3. **[True]** "The old troop transport out on the Tide Flats still carries
   its dead at low tide." The *Aldermoor* (`R5a`; `G5`'s Tideline
   Lingerer).
4. **[False]** "There's a ward against drowning that actually works, if a
   Hollowmere fisherman says it over you." Harmless local colour; no play
   effect (`R5b` §5).
5. **[True]** "Wren Catchpole will put anyone up at the Anchorage who can
   pay." (`R5a`, D-26)
6. **[Open]** "Something official comes to Fallow Head if you disturb it."
   True once the party actually disturbs it — Brother Prynne, dispatched
   late and arriving alone (`R5b`, D-27). Don't reveal this before it's
   earned.
7. **[True]** "Every marker behind the First House has a number, and most
   of them never got a name." The Named Ground (`R5a`, D-26; see Handout
   5).
8. **[False]** "The First House burned the same year as Ascension House."
   It didn't — decommissioned decades earlier, kept standing as an archive
   (`R5a`, D-26).

## Encounter Tables

Each table assumes the region's Pest Pressure Clock (`C2` Clock 5) is
either **low** or **high**. At low, treat any Pest-class-horror result as
Social instead — per `G1`'s own instruction, this clock is weather, never a
boss fight, and shouldn't fire when nothing has disturbed the ground.
Archetype write-ups are in the matching `G` file; nothing here needed a
number.

### Region I — Larrow Valley (d8)

1–4 Social (a Valley Trust Functionary, the Capping-Camp Tester, or
whichever named NPC the scene calls for) · 5 Pursuit (a company runner,
reporting to Corlett) · 6 Arcana, optional — skip if no PC has the hook ·
7 Church operatives (Brothers Aldous and Tam, north road) · 8 Pest-class
horror, high only (the Collapsed-Tunnel Lingerer — mine workings or Hessel
Bight's cellar, `G1`).

### Region II — Vantry (d8)

1–4 Social (Wick's tell, Hester Combe, Corde Ashby, Ress Tannery) ·
5 Pursuit (a Church-adjacent face, reporting to Wick) · 6 Arcana, optional ·
7 Church operatives (a Mercy sister or brother on the bread line) ·
8 Pest-class horror, high only (the Rail-Cut Lingerer, `G2`). **Fourth Mercy
products** (the Tank-Four Remnant, if the DM has made it real) isn't a roll
result — place it, at most once, inside Ascension House's own burned
footprint, never elsewhere (`G2`).

### Region III — Carrowgate (d8)

1–4 Social (Sorrel, Pell's tell, Vane's tell, Caddock Lowe's tenants) ·
5 Pursuit (a retained inquiry agent, reporting to Pell) · 6 Arcana, optional ·
7 Church operatives (a Mercy sibling at the Mercy House, or a Sanctum clerk
asked a direct question) · 8 Pest-class horror, high only (the Kept-Ground
Lingerer, `G3`). No Fourth Mercy products — the Sanctum is clean in this
region, deliberately (`R3b`, D-23).

### Region IV — Lissom Fen (d8)

1–4 Social (Pledge, Rede, Anstey, Teyla Dunmore's grief) · 5 Pursuit (Ness,
asking after visitors lightly and without suspicion in his own voice) ·
6 Arcana, optional · 7 Church operatives **or** Fourth Mercy products
(DM's choice: Sister Anstey's genuine charity at the House's public face, or
the Tanks/Early Growth if the scene is in the undercroft itself, `G4`) ·
8 Pest-class horror, high only (the Fen-Line Lingerer, diffuse rather than
concentrated, `G4`).

### Region V — the Old Coast

`R5b`'s own DM notes are explicit that this Part's danger is informational,
not physical, and that reaching for a roll here is reaching for the wrong
tool — honour that rather than filling out a full table to match the
others. If a scene genuinely needs filling: 1–6 Social (Catchpole, the
Hollowmere elder, Vance herself) · 7 Arcana, optional (a fisherman's half-
remembered ward) · 8 Pest-class horror, high only (the Tideline Lingerer,
bound to the *Aldermoor*'s wreck and the tide, `G5`). No Pursuit, no Church
operatives beyond Vance and Prynne — both are named scenes (`R5b` §6), never
random encounters — and no Fourth Mercy products.

## Handouts

Hand these over as written; the chapter citations in brackets are for the
DM, not the player.

**1. The fourth line.** Ascension House's novice-height catechism, as
photographed among the standing archive's plates or found scorched but
legible on a fallen board in the ruin itself — either way the DM's call for
how the party finds it (`hooks.md` #3 or #4).

> *bread*
> *shelter*
> *absolution*
>
> *— and, beneath, in a later, ledger-style hand —*
>
> *and ascension, by consent.*

*Resolves `hooks.md` #6 — the wording the novel withheld. Session default,
D-41 below. Play it exactly as sincere as everything else the Church says
in this campaign (canon §3): nobody wrote this to sound sinister, and that
is the point.*

**2. The standing bounty notice.** Posted at Ammet Ford's company office
and copied to Beddoe's slate (`H1`; `D1`; `R1b` → Reveal C, Route 2).

> *TWO CROWNS, PAID IN HAND.*
> *For word of anyone asking after old company papers — surveys, well
> records, anything from the works' early years.*
> *Come to the company office, Ammet Ford. Nothing you say goes further
> than this door.*
> *— Larrow Consolidated Works*

*The company runner who pays this out genuinely doesn't know what it's
for; played straight, he can be moved to say who gave the instruction,
never to explain a reason he was never given (`G1` — the Valley Trust
Functionary).*

**3. The wanted notice.** Posted at Callow Ford's board, the same board as
the capping schedule (`R1a`). The name is invented; it is not Lloyd's.

> *WANTED — EDWIN MARSH*
> *Sought for assault and theft at the Ammet Ford stores. Reward offered
> through the company office.*
> *Tall, quiet-spoken. Not from these parts by his talk, though he's lived
> here years. Last seen near Harrow's Weir.*

*The village knows this isn't what it looks like and mostly keeps that to
itself (`R1a`). Session default — the name and charge, D-41 below; nothing
about Lloyd's actual history appears on the page he's wanted on, by
design.*

**4. A quartermaster's page.** Edie's own convention (`source-bible.md` →
Objects; generalised by `F1`), for any NPC quartermaster costing a party's
downtime for real rather than waving it through.

> *Paid out — six days, on the road.*
> *Board and bed — cheap*
> *Boots, resoled — cheap*
> *Word to the capping camp — a favour, not coin*
> *Physic, a cough that wouldn't quit — moderate*
> *Closed out, and one favour owed.*

*Costs are shaped, not numbered, per `F1`'s own convention — `(SYS?)` for
the real ruleset's figures. The shape is the point: everything itemised,
nothing rounded off, exactly as Edie's own ledger reads in ch. 19.*

**5. A Named Ground marker.** One of sixty years' worth behind the First
House (`R5a`, D-26). Hand this over alone, with nothing else — it is the
whole handout.

> *No. 214.*
> *Received. Filed. Not claimed.*

*No name, because most of them never got one. This is `canon.md` §7's
"catalogued, not lurid" rule proven directly rather than cited — if a
player asks what happened to No. 214, the honest answer is that nobody
wrote it down.*

## DM Notes

**These tables are a convenience, not an obligation.** Every `Ra`/`Rb` file
above already plays Social as the default register; rolling should fill a
quiet moment, never override a scene the table is already in. Region V's
table is deliberately the thinnest, on purpose — matching, not padding,
`R5b`'s own restraint.

**Handouts work because they're not explained.** Hand over Handout 1 and
let the party sit with it; don't narrate what it implies. The same goes for
all five — per `A2`'s standing instruction, the lead web's own routes are
where implications get confirmed, not the handout text itself.

**Nothing above resolves an open question this campaign has deliberately
left open.** The Selwick discrepancy (rumour, Region I #5), the Tank-Four
Remnant (Region II encounter note), and Fourth Mercy reach beyond what's
confirmed (Region III rumour #8) all stay exactly as unresolved as
`hooks.md` and the matching `DECISIONS.md` entries left them.
